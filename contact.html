Frequently asked questions

Is this an official career counselling service?

No — Trailhead is a student-built reference project. It's a good first stop for exploring options, but for anything binding (subject choices, applications, exams) talk to your school counsellor or a qualified advisor too.

Why do some careers show more than one stream?

A handful of careers can be reached from more than one stream — for example, an Actuary pathway lists both Science and Commerce, since either combination of subjects can qualify you for it.

What does "Vocational" mean here?

Vocational careers are trained through India's National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF) — shorter, skill-first courses that get you job-ready without a full degree. They're a legitimate, often faster route into a trade.

The steps for my career sound outdated. What do I do?

Entrance exams, course durations and institution names change often. Use the contact form above to flag it, and always double-check specifics with the relevant institution before deciding anything.

Can I suggest a career that isn't listed?

Yes — that's exactly what the "suggesting a career" option in the contact form is for.
