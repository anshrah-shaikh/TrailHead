Built to be explored, not memorised

Career exploration should feel more like opening a map than studying a textbook. Browse, compare, click around and follow whatever catches your attention.

  1. 1

    Search or filter

    Type a career, or narrow the full list down by stream — Science, Commerce, Arts, Vocational or Any.

  2. 2

    Open the trail card

    Every career has a plain-language description and the qualification steps laid out like waypoints, in order.

  3. 3

    Compare a few

    Keep the tab open, open two or three more, and see which pathway actually sounds like your kind of Tuesday.
